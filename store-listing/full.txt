Daygle AI Camera is the mobile companion for your self-hosted Daygle AI Camera server. Watch your cameras live, find exactly what happened with AI-powered search, play back recordings and get instant alerts — all from your own server, with no cloud account required.

Note: this app requires a Daygle AI Camera server that you run yourself. It does not work on its own or with other camera brands.

LIVE VIEW
• See all your cameras at a glance with live snapshot thumbnails and status
• Tap any camera for a full-screen live feed
• Pinch to zoom up to 5x and pan around the picture

FIND ANY EVENT
• Browse your full detection history, loaded as you scroll
• Filter by date, camera, object label, AI tag or recognised face
• Ask AI Search: type a question in plain English, like "red car in the driveway yesterday afternoon"
• Jump straight from an event to its annotated snapshot or its recording

AI THAT EXPLAINS WHAT HAPPENED
• Read a one-sentence description of each event, written by your server's local vision model
• See extra objects your server tagged, such as a parcel, ladder or hi-vis vest
• See recognised people, unknown faces and whether the AI verified or filtered an alert

RECORDINGS AND TIMELINE
• Smooth playback of saved clips with a full scrubber and pinch-to-zoom
• Clips that are still being written show as "Preparing…" and become playable on their own
• Narrow the activity timeline to clips that triggered an alert

INSTANT ALERTS
• Real-time object and sound detection alerts, even while the app is in the background
• Alerts are delivered through your server's ntfy topic, with no Google or Firebase push service involved
• Tap an alert to open the event that triggered it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Connects directly to your own server over your local network or HTTPS
• Your video and events stay on your server
• Supports Cloudflare Access service tokens and custom request headers for servers behind a proxy

MADE FOR ANDROID
• Native, modern Material design
• Light and dark themes
• 12 or 24-hour time
• Android 8.0 or newer
